"What's your situation right now?" — Card Phrases (English)


Big cards (3 — most common situations)
────────────────────────────────────────

1) 🌅  I lost track of where I left off
       → Resume

2) 💭  This chat is too long — I can't scroll back to find anything
       → Compress

3) 🧱  About to make a big change. Don't want to break what's working
       → Backup


Small lines (5 — bottom of modal)
────────────────────────────────────────

       → Anchors

5) 🧭  Not sure what to do next
       → Next

6) 🚨  The AI just made a mess — I want to roll back
       → Restore
