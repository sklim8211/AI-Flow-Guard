# Rule

QQ Sidecar's primary market is the **English-speaking indie / maker / AI-power-user audience** (Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit `r/ChatGPT` / `r/ClaudeAI` / `r/productivity`, Indie Hackers, Twitter "building in public"). Korea is not the target market and is explicitly de-prioritized.

# Why

Founder is a Korean indie maker who knows the local market firsthand and judges it a poor fit for paid digital tooling: weak willingness to pay for software, strong free-tool default, distrust of solo Korean SaaS even when foreign-made tools are trusted. They have explicitly chosen the English market and asked us to align.

# How to apply

- When discussing distribution, pricing, copy, or positioning, default to English-market norms (direct tone, confident value statements, $9–19 one-time license territory, Lemon Squeezy / Paddle / Stripe as payment options).
- Do not pitch Korean channels (네이버 카페, 카카오, 한국 PG) or Korean-market features unless the founder reopens that direction.
- Do not duplicate-translate Korean marketing copy; English copy should be written fresh in the target tone, not translated.
- The founder is the domain expert on the product itself — defer to them on what the product *means* and what's worth saying about it. Copywriting help from us is welcomed (they pushed back on the "outsource it" framing), but the message must come from their lived use, not our guesses. Wait for them to come back with usage observations before drafting landing copy.
- Communication with the founder stays in Korean; only the product-facing copy is English.
